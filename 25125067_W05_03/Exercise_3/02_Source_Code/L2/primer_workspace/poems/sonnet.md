At Fenway, dusk leans softly on the wall,
The green left field receives the fading light;
The crowd begins its slow, expectant call,
And every inning sharpens into night.
The score may swing like weather over bay,
Yet memory keeps its bright and patient flame;
The old songs rise with every crack of play,
And each new season answers to the same.
From peskier days to summers deep and long,
The Red Sox carry grit in woven thread;
They teach the faithful how to sing with song,
And how to hope when lesser hopes have fled.
When final outs draw close and shadows blend,
Boston still believes the game can mend.
